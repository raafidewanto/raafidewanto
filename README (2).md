## About me

I'm **Muhamad Raafi Dewanto**, an Information Systems graduate from **Gunadarma University** and a **Hacktiv8 Full Stack JavaScript** bootcamp graduate. I build web and mobile applications, with a particular interest in the APIs and databases that make them work.

I work with **JavaScript and TypeScript** across the stack — from React and Next.js interfaces to Node.js and NestJS backends. My projects include REST and GraphQL APIs, database integration, authentication, and real-time features.

I enjoy turning practical ideas into working products and learning through hands-on development. My retail experience also shaped the way I communicate, collaborate, and pay attention to detail.

<details>

- 🎓 **Information Systems, Gunadarma University** — my academic foundation in technology and business.
- 💻 **Hacktiv8 Full Stack JavaScript** — hands-on experience building web, mobile, and backend projects.
- 🩸 **VitaFlux** — my final bootcamp project, where I contributed as a full stack developer to a mobile blood donation platform.
- ⚽ **Retail & football specialist experience** — strengthened my communication, teamwork, and attention to detail.
